## Rules

- Prefer SVG for diagrams and logos; include a PNG fallback.
- Do not embed fonts you do not have a license for.
- Name files descriptively: `gcaii-logo-horizontal.svg`.
- Record the source and license of any third-party asset alongside the asset.
- The name-and-use note in [`NAME.md`](../NAME.md) applies.
