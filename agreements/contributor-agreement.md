You represent that:

- you created the contribution, or have the right to submit it;

- it contains no confidential information or personal data you are not authorized to publish;
- you are not subject to an agreement that conflicts with this grant.

## 4. Sign-off

Every commit includes:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

The sign-off certifies the Developer Certificate of Origin, version 1.1: <https://developercertificate.org/>.

## 5. No employment or agency

Contribution does not create employment, partnership, or agency. Contributors are not entitled to compensation.

## 6. Attribution

Contributors are credited in the repository history and, where appropriate, in release notes. You may request removal of your name from future credits; the license grant survives.

## 7. Withdrawal

You may stop contributing at any time. Contributions already distributed remain licensed.

This template is not legal advice. Before accepting contributions on behalf of an institution, have the terms reviewed by someone authorized to do so.
