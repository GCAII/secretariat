# Institutional Alliances and Participation

> **Status:** Active · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-08
> **Replaces:** OSF wiki page `wiki/alliances`
>
> **No formal partnerships are currently established.** This document describes intended participation, not an existing affiliation. It is non-binding.

## 1. Purpose

Describe how independent researchers, labs, and commercial teams can participate in GCAII without implying partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement.

## 2. Participation tracks

| Track | Who | Contribution | Commitment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Observer** | Anyone | Read, comment, file issues | None |
| **Contributor** | Researchers, engineers | Patches, test vectors, reviews | DCO sign-off |
| **Node** | Labs, independent operators | Run the harness, publish results | Voluntary |
| **Liaison** | Institutions, companies | Joint research, review, hosting | Non-binding MOU |

## 3. Open-science interoperability

Participants align around public artifacts, open registries, and persistent identifiers. Proprietary implementations are welcome; the open-science expectation applies to results contributed to the public record, not to internal code.

## 4. Edge resource federation

- Nodes contribute validation results voluntarily.
- Runtime data stays local; hardware stays isolated.
- Diverse silicon is welcome: embedded, legacy, and commercial edge accelerators.

## 5. Knowledge transfer

- Specifications, mitigation scripts, benchmarks, and audit frameworks are publicly accessible.
- Security-sensitive findings may be embargoed until a fix is available, then published.
- No contributor is required to disclose proprietary model weights.

## 6. Governance

- Technical evolution follows the RFC process and rough consensus.
- Participation does not confer a vote beyond the maintainer process.
- A liaison does not create a board seat or a veto.

## 7. Local amendments

An adopting institution may adapt the guidance locally, provided it preserves zero-telemetry, PII handling, and deterministic verification. It must state the upstream version and the deviation.

## 8. Publicity

Do not describe GCAII as a partner, sponsor, or certifier without written approval. Factual statements such as "contributed test vectors" are permitted.

## 9. Term

Participation ends when a participant stops contributing or gives notice. Public artifacts already released remain available.
