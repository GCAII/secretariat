# Institutional Liaison Terms

> **Status:** Active · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-08

Template for research groups, labs, and companies that want to collaborate with GCAII.

> **Non-binding.** This document describes intended collaboration. It is not a contract, partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement. A binding agreement requires signatures from authorized representatives.

## 1. Purpose

Enable joint research, independent validation, or deployment feedback without implying formal affiliation.

## 2. Scope

Possible activities:

- running the conformance harness and sharing results;
- contributing test vectors or hardware profiles;
- co-authoring a research artifact;
- reviewing a draft specification;
- hosting a mirror or archive.

## 3. What this is not

- Not a partnership, joint venture, or legal entity.
- Not a sponsorship or funding commitment.
- Not an endorsement of either party's products.
- Not a grant of any right in either party's name.

## 4. Publicity

Neither party may describe the other as a "partner," "sponsor," or "certified" without written approval. Factual statements ("contributed test vectors for GCAII-STD-0001") are permitted.

## 5. Data and confidentiality

- Runtime data stays with the party that owns it.
- Shared artifacts are public unless a separate confidentiality agreement says otherwise.
- Personal data is not exchanged without a lawful basis.

## 6. Intellectual property

- Each party retains its own IP.
- Contributions are licensed under the repository's terms in `LICENSING.md`; at 1.0.0 that is CC BY 4.0.
- No transfer of ownership is implied.

## 7. Term and termination

The liaison continues until either party ends it in writing. Public artifacts already released remain available.

## 8. Dispute resolution

Parties will attempt good-faith resolution. Nothing here creates a legal obligation to arbitrate or litigate.
