# Agreements

> **Status:** Active · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-08

Templates for contribution and collaboration.

> **Important:** GCAII is currently unincorporated. These documents are templates, not executed contracts. Anything described as an MOU is explicitly non-binding until reviewed and signed by parties with authority to bind their organizations. This is not legal advice.

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [`contributor-agreement.md`](contributor-agreement.md) | License grant and contribution terms |
| [`institutional-liaison.md`](institutional-liaison.md) | Non-binding collaboration terms |
| [`mou-template.md`](mou-template.md) | MOU skeleton |
| [`alliances.md`](alliances.md) | Participation framework (replaces OSF `wiki/alliances`) |

## Rule

Do not describe a liaison as a partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement unless a signed document says so and the other party has approved the wording.
