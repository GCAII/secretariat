# Name and Use

> **Status:** Active · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-08

## What the name is

"Global Constrained AI Initiative" and the initials "GCAII" are a descriptive name for this project. They are not a trademark.

- No trademark is registered, and none is claimed.
- No application is planned.
- No exclusive right is claimed to the letters GCAII, in any combination, in any jurisdiction.
- Nothing here grants permission, because permission is not this project's to give.

The name identifies a body of work. It does not identify a source of goods or services, and it is not enforced.

## What is asked of others

Two requests, not conditions:

1. Do not state or imply that this project endorses, certifies, partners with, or is affiliated with you or your organisation when it does not.
2. If you modify `GCAII-STD-0001`, say so, and do not present the modified text as the published standard.

Neither request depends on trademark law. They are about not misrepresenting what this project is. This project has no legal entity, no counsel, and no enforcement mechanism, and does not pretend otherwise.

## Other users of the same initials

The initials GCAII are also used by **Green Converged AI Infrastructure LLC** (Georgetown, Massachusetts, United States), a commercial AI-infrastructure company at `gcaii.com`.

This project is not affiliated with that company, is not endorsed by it, and does not endorse it. There is no shared ownership, staff, funding, or governance. That is a statement of fact about this project; it is not a claim about anyone else's rights, and it is not an assertion that the letters are unavailable to anyone.

The same applies to any other organisation that uses the initials. This project does not police the acronym and has no standing to.

## Referring to the standard

You may say that an implementation "implements GCAII-STD-0001" or is "self-assessed against GCAII-STD-0001 (draft)" and link to your evidence.

Do not imply verification by a third party until a verified conformance report exists. See [`programs/conformance.md`](programs/conformance.md).

## Why this is not a trademark policy

This project is unincorporated and unfunded. There is no legal person to own a mark, no budget to register one, and no intention to enforce one. Writing a trademark policy would claim rights that do not exist and cannot be defended.

What is left is the part that is real: a statement of who this project is not, and a request not to misrepresent it.
