# Appeals

> **Status:** Active · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-08

## 1. Grounds

An appeal may be filed for:

- a procedural error;
- a factual error in the evidence review;
- an undisclosed conflict of interest;
- a remedy disproportionate to the finding.

Disagreement with a technical result is not, by itself, grounds. New reproducible evidence is.

## 2. Filing

Open a conformance dispute issue using the template. Include the report ID, the specification version, the grounds, and the evidence. Do not include confidential data in public.

## 3. Timeline

| Step | Time target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | 7 days |
| Panel appointment | 14 days |
| Decision | 30 days |

## 4. Panel

The panel consists of maintainers not involved in the original decision. If none are uninvolved, an external advisor is invited. The panel may request a re-run of the harness.

## 5. Decision

Possible outcomes:

- **Upheld** — the original decision stands.
- **Overturned** — the decision is reversed and the registry updated.
- **Remanded** — the matter returns for a new review with instructions.

The decision is recorded as an ADR and published, with personal data redacted.

## 6. No retaliation

Filing an appeal in good faith is protected. Retaliation is a code-of-conduct violation.

## 7. Finality

A panel decision is final for that report. New evidence may support a new submission, not a reopening of the same appeal without cause.
