# Programs

> **Status:** Active · **Version:** 1.0.0 · **Date:** 2026-11-08

Programs define how conformance is verified, audited, and disputed. They are process documents; the technical requirements live in [`specs/`](../specs/).

| Program | Purpose | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [`conformance.md`](conformance.md) | How a report is submitted, reviewed, and recorded | Active |
| [`audit.md`](audit.md) | Who may audit, and how independence is maintained | Active |
| [`appeals.md`](appeals.md) | How a decision can be challenged | Active |

## Separation of concerns

- **Specification** says what must be true.
- **Conformance** says how evidence is produced and checked.
- **Audit** says who may check it independently.
- **Appeals** says what happens when the process is wrong.

## No fees, no membership

There is no paid tier. Conformance is technical. A donation, if ever accepted, does not affect a finding.
